01 · Welcome, explorer

A painting is only the beginning.

Welcome to Super Mormon, a free 3D Book of Mormon adventure game. Run through ancient streets, follow the iron rod, help build a ship, and discover places worth taking the long way around.

This is a game of movement, exploration, and helping people. Different adventures ask you to navigate, carry supplies, climb, herd, sneak, or time your next move. Read the current objective, look around, and give things a try.

A note before you begin

This independent game is in development. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Places, costumes, dialogue, and many player activities are imaginative adaptations. The scripture is the source; the game is an invitation to explore it.

02 · Getting started

Make the gallery your home.

  1. Open the game. Use a browser with JavaScript and WebGL support. On a phone, turn sideways for more room.
  2. Take a look around. You begin in a recreation of the Conference Center. Practice moving and turning the camera before setting out.
  3. Find the first painting. Take a running jump into Jerusalem Streets. Finish stories in order to open the next adventure; completed stories can be replayed.
  4. Follow the objective. The objective tells you what to do next. A nearby action prompt tells you when you can talk, search, carry, or use something.
  5. Come back and explore. The home button or the pause menu returns you to the gallery. Later paintings are along side hallways and upper floors.
Try the garden

Find six gold tokens and carry the practice bundle to the gold ring. These optional challenges are a good way to learn the controls.

Take the scenic route

Explore the Conference Center and roof gardens. The auditorium is for exploring; story entrances and objectives are elsewhere.

03 · Know your controls

A few buttons. Many possibilities.

In the move descriptions, Jump, Action, and Crawl mean the controls below. The game’s button hints change with your input device.

Keyboard, touch, and standard gamepad controls

Move WASD or arrow keys Left virtual stick Left stick
Jump Space A A / bottom face button
Action E B B / right face button
Crawl Hold Shift or Z Tap Z to toggle Hold LT
Look around Drag the game view; Q / C orbit left / right Drag the view outside the controls Right stick
Recenter camera R ↻ button LB
Pause Escape or P Ⅱ button Start / menu button

Gamepad lettering varies. These are the game’s standard browser mappings; an adapter may report buttons differently.

Playing with an N64-style controller?

Open Pause → Settings → Set up N64 controller and press the buttons shown. The game remembers your mapping in this browser. Use the stick to move, A to jump, B for action, Z to crawl, R to recenter, and Start to pause. C-left / C-right turn the camera; C-up / C-down change camera distance.

04 · The movement handbook

There is more than one way up.

Practice on open ground first. Move relative to the camera, and recenter it when you need a clearer view of the next landing.

↑

Run & jump

Move, then press Jump. Hold Jump for a higher leap; release earlier for a shorter one.

↗

Long jump

Get a running start, then press Crawl + Jump. Use the extra distance to cross gaps.

↶

Backflip

Stand still, hold Crawl, and press Jump. Leave room behind you.

↑↑↑

Double & triple jump

Keep running and press Jump again as you land. Chain another landing jump for the triple. These are ground-to-air combinations, not extra jumps in midair.

↔

Side somersault

While running, reverse direction and jump during the turn.

↖

Wall kick

Jump against a solid wall, then press Jump again while near it to kick away.

↓

Crawl & ground pound

Use Crawl while moving to get low. In the air, press Crawl for a ground pound.

⌃

Catch a ledge

Jump toward a reachable edge. Move toward it or press Jump to pull up; Crawl lets go.

↟

Climb

Jump toward a climbable tree or pole. Move up / down to climb; Jump pushes away and Crawl lets go. Not every tree is a climbing route.

◇

Carry & throw

Action picks up a nearby carryable bundle. Action again throws it; Crawl + Action sets it down.

On touch screens, Z stays toggled until you tap it again. Turn it off when you want to run normally. For an in-game refresher, choose Pause → Moves & practice.

05 · Your 26 adventures

Choose a painting. Step into a story.

The gallery opens these adventures in order. These introductions leave the discoveries to you. Each scripture link opens the relevant source chapter; chapter ranges name the broader reading.

I · Jerusalem & the wilderness

  1. Jerusalem Streets

    Find your bearings among the streets and passages. Learn to look around before rushing ahead.

    1 Nephi 3
    Need a starting hint?

    Look for the indigo doorway around the western homes. Turn around the outside of the dwelling at the cross street to find its entrance.

  2. Laban’s Palace

    Explore the palace setting as the brothers seek the plates of brass.

    1 Nephi 3
    Need a starting hint?

    Head to the far end of the courtyard and approach Laban. After the encounter, return to your brothers.

  3. Lehi’s House

    Search the family’s imagined home during the effort to obtain the plates.

    1 Nephi 3
    Need a starting hint?

    Search every room for the five family treasures, including upstairs. When you have them all, return to the courtyard entrance.

  4. The Treasure Offer

    Follow the brothers’ attempt to exchange their property for the record.

    1 Nephi 3
    Need a starting hint?

    Bring the treasures to Laban first. When the objective changes, keep your brothers with you as you head for the city gate.

  5. Jerusalem by Night

    Return through the city after dark. Watch your route and the people around you.

    1 Nephi 4
    Need a starting hint?

    Begin by searching the moonlit streets for Laban. Read the next objective after the encounter rather than heading straight for the plates.

  6. The Tree of Life

    Enter Lehi’s vision, follow the iron rod, and discover its invitation to share joy.

    1 Nephi 8; 11–15
    Need a starting hint?

    After the opening scene, walk forward from your starting position toward the narrow path and its iron rod. When the prompt appears, hold Action to grasp the rod, and keep holding it as you move along the path through the mist.

  7. The Broken Bow

    Help provide food for the family. Follow directions into the mountain and take care with your aim.

    1 Nephi 16
    Need a starting hint?

    Your first task is target practice on the mountain. Follow the trail up before looking for food for the family; the objective will change as the story unfolds.

  8. Building the Ship

    Explore Bountiful and help with the work of building an imagined vessel.

    1 Nephi 17–18
    Need a starting hint?

    Begin with the mountain trail and the prayer prompt. The ore, tools, and timber tasks come afterward, in the order shown by the objective.

  9. The Ocean Voyage

    Explore life aboard the ship through an imagined child’s visit as the voyage unfolds.

    1 Nephi 18
    Need a starting hint?

    Find Laman and Lemuel below deck first. Look for the stairs between deck levels rather than trying to jump through the deck.

  10. A New Home

    Explore the shore and lead willing families through the valleys to a new settlement. This scene condenses years.

    2 Nephi 4–5
    Need a starting hint?

    Explore inland from the shore and look for Lehi. Later, when the families follow, take the valley route toward the blue beacon.

II · Care, testimony & service

  1. The Olive Vineyard

    Work among the trees in a playable adaptation of the allegory. Attend to the task at hand.

    Jacob 5
    Need a starting hint?

    Find the servant in the olive garden before beginning the tree work. Follow the current task rather than trying to tend every tree at once.

  2. Jacob and Sherem

    Explore an imagined settlement and find Jacob as questions of faith come before the people.

    Jacob 7
    Need a starting hint?

    Look for Sherem among people in the city and its outskirts. Follow the changing objective as the encounter develops.

  3. The Waters of Mormon

    Find Alma in secret. Look for a safe way through the landscape and avoid the spies.

    Mosiah 18
    Need a starting hint?

    Watch the spies’ turning heads from behind a wall. Cross the open space when they look away; stay alert for the next safe place to wait.

  4. Ammon and the King’s Flocks

    Help with the king’s flocks. Watch how your position guides animals where they need to go.

    Alma 17–18
    Need a starting hint?

    Begin by guiding the flock toward the water at Sebus. Stand on the side away from the water and adjust your position in small steps.

III · Courage & deliverance

  1. The Title of Liberty

    Climb through the city and help Moroni rally the people.

    Alma 46
    Need a starting hint?

    Find the quiet prayer court first. After the opening sequence, follow the objective to the climbing route through the city.

  2. Lehonti’s Mountain

    Race up the mountain in an imagined effort to warn Lehonti. The historical outcome is remembered, not rewritten.

    Alma 47
    Need a starting hint?

    Follow the mountain’s spiral route all the way up to Lehonti’s camp. Cutting across the slope is not a substitute for learning the route.

  3. The Narrow Pass

    Hear a warning, consult a scout, and hurry to the pass ahead of Morianton’s group.

    Alma 50:25–36
    Need a starting hint?

    Hear the maidservant’s warning, then find Teancum’s scout beside the western ridge. Plan your route to the pass before the race.

  4. Teancum by Night

    Navigate past sentries and find your way back to camp. A quiet route is more useful than a hasty one.

    Alma 51:32–37
    Need a starting hint?

    Meet your servant before leaving camp. Watch the sentries and use the stones and trees to plan a quiet crossing.

  5. The Prisoners of Gid

    Help carry out the rescue plan. Pay attention to deliveries and the route across the wall.

    Alma 55
    Need a starting hint?

    As Laman, receive Moroni’s orders before approaching the guards. Later deliveries use different parts of the wall, so reread each objective.

  6. The Walls of Nephihah

    Scout the city, gather materials, and help prepare a way over its defenses.

    Alma 62
    Need a starting hint?

    Start by scouting from the western wall and looking toward the eastern entrance. Return to the army before gathering ladder materials.

IV · A promise & a gathering

  1. Samuel the Lamanite

    Climb the city wall, deliver the message, and find a way out.

    Helaman 13–16
    Need a starting hint?

    Look for the outside ledges leading up the city wall. Line up each jump with the camera before you climb higher.

  2. The Night Without Darkness

    Visit a town waiting for the promised sign. Help its people as the night approaches.

    3 Nephi 1
    Need a starting hint?

    Head for the gathering square beside the waiting families. The west stairs lead toward the rooftop watcher when that objective appears.

  3. Lachoneus’s Gathering

    Help a community gather and prepare. Think about routes and the supplies people need.

    3 Nephi 3–4
    Need a starting hint?

    Find the refuge granary and note its entrances. Carry provision loads there, then help guide the southern family along a clear route.

  4. Christ at Bountiful

    Explore an imagined temple precinct and approach the sacred gathering with reverence.

    3 Nephi 8–11; 17
    Need a starting hint?

    Approach the gathering near the temple and listen. Optional rooms and garden discoveries do not unlock or earn the sacred encounter.

V · Shared lives & entrusted records

  1. A People of Peace

    Explore a rebuilt city, fit timber sections into a garden bridge, and carry shared seeds across the span.

    4 Nephi 1
    Need a starting hint?

    Timbers wait in the two western workshops. Fit each at the western abutment. The ford offers another way around, but take the shared seeds across the bridge you restored.

  2. Mormon and the Hidden Records

    Choose a path up the hill to the record chamber, then bring its secured carrier down to the valley refuge.

    Mormon 1:1–5; 2:17; 4:23; 6:6
    Need a starting hint?

    Broad stairs flank the central ridge. The carrier needs both hands, so use the stairs on your return. Watch the spillway warning, or take the dry eastern bank.

06 · A little help

Look twice. Try another path.

Getting stuck is part of exploring. These hints offer a nudge without giving away the whole route.

I cannot enter the next painting.

Finish the earlier unfinished story first. Paintings unlock in order, and completed stories stay available for replay. Later paintings are in side hallways and on upper floors.

I am beside the right person, but nothing happens.

Read the current objective. Move closer and look for an Action prompt; you may need to finish a task or set down a carried object first.

I keep missing a jump.

Recenter the camera, line up on flat ground, and leave space for a running start. Hold Jump for height. Use a long jump for distance, and practice landing before adding jump combinations.

A guard or spy keeps spotting me.

Pause to watch the patrol, use cover, and choose when to cross open ground. Crawling can help you move carefully; it does not make you invisible. A retry is a chance to try another route.

I cannot get a bundle to its destination.

Read the delivery prompt and bring the bundle close to the target. Crawl + Action places it down gently; Action on its own throws it.

The animals keep moving away.

Try approaching from the side opposite the destination. Make small changes to your position rather than chasing straight through the flock.

I have lost my way.

Reread the objective and look for landmarks, signs, or a task marker where the adventure provides one. Turn the camera and check side routes. Pause → Restart this scene starts the current adventure over.

07 · For families & scripture readers

Play a story. Then open the book.

Looking for an LDS or Mormon video game, a Book of Mormon game, or something to play together on a Sunday? Super Mormon is made for families who enjoy scripture-inspired exploration. Families can decide how it fits their own Sabbath traditions.

The game is designed with older children in mind. Later adventures ask for more navigation, timing, stealth, and planning. Stories include danger, hunting, war, death, and sacred events; a parent may want to accompany younger players. This is an audience description, not an official age rating.

Try taking turns: one player handles the controls while another reads the objective or spots a path. After an adventure, read its source chapter together. Ask, “Which details came from the scripture? Which were imagined for the game? What did the people choose to do?”

Scripture & imagination

The chapter links above point to the Book of Mormon on the Church’s scripture website. Dialogue, terrain, architecture, clothing, routes, and some player roles are invented or condensed. The vineyard is an allegory; Lehi’s tree is a vision. The ship’s design and ancient geography are not established reconstructions. Optional discoveries are game activities, not requirements added to sacred events.

08 · Keep your adventure going

A bookmark for next time.

What gets saved?
Completed stories are saved automatically in this browser when storage is available. Return using the same browser, device, and site address. There is no account or cloud synchronization. Do not expect your exact position or unfinished scene tasks to resume.
Can I replay or restart?
Yes. Reenter a completed painting to replay it. Pause → Restart this scene restarts the current scene; Pause → Return to the gallery takes you home. Your completed-story record remains.
Why did my progress disappear?
Clearing site data, using a different browser or device, or leaving a private browsing session can remove or separate saves. Browser storage restrictions can also prevent saving.
Can I change sound or graphics?
Open Pause → Settings. Sound can be turned off. Balanced targets 60 FPS, Battery saver uses 30 FPS and a softer picture, and High refresh follows your display. Actual performance depends on your device.
The game is slow or will not load.
Check your connection and try again. Use an up-to-date browser with WebGL enabled. Try Battery saver, close other demanding tabs, or refresh the page. Refreshing restarts the current session; completed stories remain if browser storage is working.
My controller is not responding.
Connect it, focus the game, and press a button. For N64-style hardware, use the setup in Settings. Adapter support varies; keyboard and touch controls are also available.
Does the game collect statistics?
The published game uses Google Analytics to improve play. See Privacy & gameplay statistics for details. This booklet does not load analytics or the game engine.
Can I print this manual?
Use your browser’s Print command or Save as PDF. The booklet includes a print layout, with the hints expanded for paper.

Found a problem? Send feedback on GitHub. Include the adventure name, what happened, and your device and browser.
