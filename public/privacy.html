Privacy & gameplay statistics

Super Mormon uses Google Analytics on the published game to understand which stories, moves, camera controls and optional discoveries players use, how much active time they spend, and where they leave a story unfinished. This helps improve the game.

Statistics include level and action names, device/browser information, fullscreen mode and visit information. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits and report returning players. The game does not send names, email addresses, saved story progress, exact movement trails or dialogue text. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled.

Google receives network information when its Analytics service is used. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy. You can prevent Analytics collection with Google’s supported browser opt-out add-on or your browser’s tracking controls. Blocking Analytics does not prevent playing.

Saved progress and development

Story progress and control preferences are saved in your browser. Clearing site storage removes them. Local development, test scene links and character preview pages do not load Google Analytics. This privacy page does not load Analytics.
