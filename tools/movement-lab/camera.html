What we’re borrowing from Mario 64

Stick direction uses the camera’s horizontal reference. A curved stick response makes smaller deflections gentler. This experiment trails the player geometrically: sideways movement arcs around the camera; straight movement pulls it along. Its distance, low angle and smoothing are our tuning, not an exact Nintendo reconstruction.

Mario 64 has multiple camera modes and area-specific behavior; it does not use one universal follow rule. Reference: reconstructed input code, reconstructed camera code, movement states, Nintendo manual.

Open-floor prototype only. No game saves are read or written. Try the current-camera switch to compare; story-world camera safety remains a separate integration step.
