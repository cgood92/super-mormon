The revision follows the curved fan seating, sweeping balcony fascias, concentric cream ceiling bands, inward-facing chairs, warm wood organ case with stepped pipe towers, blue stage backdrop and side projection screens. Shapes and dimensions remain simplified for the game; no photo is redistributed as an asset.

The ground aisle now shares the seating rake, with entry stairs and an enclosed stage. Lower seating banks and stairs are filled to the foundation. Balconies have substantial filled structures and continuous cream soffits rather than exposed floating treads. Normal-input browser checks reached the stage and upper balcony after the revision. Automated tests cover all foyer entrances, aisle/seat height continuity, stair infill, guards and roof clearance.

All world signs now use textures matching their panel proportions and measured font fitting; browser checks verified the five main Conference Center sign types fit with the actual font.

Additional user-supplied front and side photographs clarified that the rostrum access stairs flank the pulpit, the center front remains continuous, and choir/presiding seating faces the audience. The updated rostrum follows those features. Balcony trim was rebuilt as adjoining cream/wood/cream bands after confirming depth fighting between coincident faces. Chair backrest offsets are rotated with the complete seat, not just each symmetric box.
