# Captain Moroni · Bronze Commander

The user selected concept **01 Bronze Commander** on October 28, 2026. The approved visual reference is [the full-body and face sheet](art/moroni-bronze-commander.png).

The defining features are heroic adult proportions, an angular clean-shaven face, short flat brown hair beneath an open bronze headband, a fitted bronze cuirass, layered plate shoulders, cream military tunic with full thigh coverage, crimson half-cloak with a shoulder clasp, bronze bracers and greaves, a tall wooden shield with bronze edging, and a sheathed sword. Rounded blue armor and the domed helmet are superseded.

This is an illustrative character design rather than a historical likeness or archaeological reconstruction. [Alma 46:12–13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/46?lang=eng) records Moroni’s coat, head-plate, breastplate, shields, and armor; the exact appearance, materials, colors, anatomy, and equipment design are artistic choices.

The shared gameplay movement, physics height, other cast members, and the Title of Liberty coat-to-banner sequence remain part of acceptance. Visual checks must include front, side and quarter views, marching, rapid reversals, crawl and recovery, both hero and NPC detail, and the actual Liberty approach and prayer scene.

## Verified implementation

The first playable pass replaces Moroni’s body armor, equipment and head while retaining the shared actor controls. Armor and clothing follow the remapped rig; crawl panels blend from torso to thigh and restore their authored standing geometry. Hero, NPC and crowd detail remain supported.

Validation: 468 automated tests pass and the production build succeeds. Rendered checks cover front, side and quarter views, marching, sharp reversals, hero/NPC crawl and recovery, and a normal approach to the Liberty prayer sequence on desktop and phone portrait framing. The playable model is intentionally stylized; the approved sheet remains the art direction reference for further iteration.

Playtest revision: removed all scalp hair at the user’s request. The red front drape and clasp sit outside the bronze breastplate, and the tunic uses continuous front/side/back thigh surfaces extending toward the knees. Eight-angle coverage checks include standing, running reversals and crawling. The original concept sheet is retained as the selection record; its hairstyle is superseded.

Hair clarification: the user requested short flat hair rather than baldness. A single close-cut brown surface now follows the scalp, with an exposed forehead and ears and no swept locks or added crown volume. Front, side and quarter renders verify the silhouette.
