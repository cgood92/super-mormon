# Captain Moroni · Bronze Commander

The user selected concept **01 Bronze Commander** on October 28, 2026. The approved visual reference is [the full-body and face sheet](art/moroni-bronze-commander.png).

The defining features are heroic adult proportions, an angular clean-shaven face, short swept brown hair beneath an open bronze headband, a fitted bronze cuirass, layered plate shoulders, cream military tunic, crimson half-cloak with a shoulder clasp, bronze bracers and greaves, a tall wooden shield with bronze edging, and a sheathed sword. Rounded blue armor and the domed helmet are superseded.

This is an illustrative character design rather than a historical likeness or archaeological reconstruction. [Alma 46:12–13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/46?lang=eng) records Moroni’s coat, head-plate, breastplate, shields, and armor; the exact appearance, materials, colors, anatomy, and equipment design are artistic choices.

The shared gameplay movement, physics height, other cast members, and the Title of Liberty coat-to-banner sequence remain part of acceptance. Visual checks must include front, side and quarter views, marching, rapid reversals, crawl and recovery, both hero and NPC detail, and the actual Liberty approach and prayer scene.
