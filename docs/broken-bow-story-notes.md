# The Broken Bow — story source and interpretation

Primary source: [1 Nephi 16:17–32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/16?lang=eng).

The text says the travelers camped to rest and seek food (verse 17); Nephi's fine steel bow broke while he went to hunt, and his brothers' bows had lost their spring (verses 18–21). Nephi made a wooden bow and a straight-stick arrow, then asked Lehi where to find food (verse 23). Lehi sought guidance; writing appeared on the ball, and Nephi followed its directions to the mountain top (verses 24–30). He obtained food from wild beasts and returned to the tents, where the family rejoiced and gave thanks (verses 31–32).

The three-shot summit practice and third-shot snap are an invented playable opening. The scripture does not say Nephi broke the bow in practice or after three shots. The separated collectibles, automatic crafting, dialogue, trail, canvas shelter form, specific animal silhouettes and species, foothill lookout, and compressed chronology are game interpretations. No campsite or mountain geography is presented as an established reconstruction. Hunting is represented without graphic injury, and the outcome of obtaining food remains fixed.
