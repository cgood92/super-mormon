# Next adventures after Jacob's allegory

Research roadmap, not an implementation specification. Sources are official scripture chapters from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Physical routes, collectibles, environmental puzzles, and errands below are proposed game adaptations; they are not additional historical claims. Keep scripture events distinct from those adaptations.

The intended feel is free exploration, optional discoveries, jumping, climbing, carrying, gathering, and escape. Prayer, revelation, baptism, testimony, and conversion should remain reverent story moments rather than combat powers, scores, or button quizzes.

## Event order and important qualifications

- Jacob 6 applies the allegory from Jacob 5. Sherem is the next narrative episode, but Jacob 7:1 explicitly places his arrival after some years; do not portray it as the next day.
- The chapter headings give approximate editorial dates, not exact timestamps for every event: Enos about 420 BC; Jarom about 399–361 BC; Zeniff about 200 BC onward; Abinadi's death about 148 BC; the Waters of Mormon about 147–145 BC; Benjamin's address and Mosiah II's accession about 124 BC; Ammon/Limhi about 121–120 BC; Alma the Younger's conversion within the approximately 100–92 BC span of Mosiah 27.
- Reading order differs from event order. Mosiah 9 begins a flashback to Zeniff's departure from Zarahemla. Mosiah 23 rewinds again to Alma's earlier departure from Noah's kingdom.
- Mosiah I, who leads the migration to Zarahemla, is Benjamin's father. Mosiah II is Benjamin's son. Distinguish them clearly in the game.
- Alma's early settlement at Helam, Limhi's people's history, and Benjamin's reign are parallel story branches. Do not imply a precise synchronization that the text does not provide. Benjamin's address precedes Ammon's expedition: Mosiah 6:4–7 describes Mosiah II's accession and three peaceful years; Mosiah 7:1 then introduces the expedition.
- Limhi's escape precedes Alma's later captivity and escape. The army pursuing Limhi becomes lost, encounters Noah's priests, and discovers Alma's people at Helam (Mosiah 22:15–16; 23:30–35). Both communities reach Zarahemla before the combined assembly in Mosiah 25.
- The Ammon who finds Limhi is a descendant of Zarahemla (Mosiah 7:3, 13), not the son of Mosiah who later serves among the Lamanites.
- Words of Mormon is a later editorial bridge, not a new chronological adventure here. Jarom and the early Omni record keepers provide sparse, compressed history; a detailed invented campaign should not be presented as scripture.

## Ten manageable adventures

### 1. Jacob and Sherem

**Scripture:** [Jacob 7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng). Sherem denies Christ, challenges Jacob, asks for a sign, and later confesses. Jacob entrusts the plates to Enos.

Game variations:

- Explore the settlement and take either the main path or an optional climbing route to reach Jacob's gathering.
- Place optional scripture discovery spots around the environment, with brief passages available for inspection rather than quiz gates.
- Close with a quiet record-handoff scene that opens the path toward Enos's forest.

Keep the confrontation and confession as witnessed story scenes. Sherem is not a boss to defeat, and the player does not cause divine judgment.

### 2. Enos in the forest

**Scripture:** [Enos 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng). Enos goes to hunt, remembers his father's teaching, prays through day and night, receives forgiveness, and prays for others and the preservation of the records.

Game variations:

- Follow animal tracks through a forest with loops, fallen logs, stepping stones, and optional overlooks.
- Find a secluded clearing using terrain and sound cues; alternative climbing routes reward curiosity with scenic discoveries.
- Let changing light carry the prayer scene from day into night, followed by a peaceful return route.

Do not make killing animals the objective or portray forgiveness as a reward earned through collectibles or timed input.

### 3. Keepers of the record and Mosiah's migration

**Scripture:** [Jarom 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jarom/1?lang=eng), [Omni 1:1–19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng). Records pass through generations. Mosiah is warned to leave the land of Nephi, leads those who follow into the wilderness, and discovers the people of Zarahemla. The brass plates are a reason for rejoicing.

Game variations:

- Use a brief record-handoff montage for Jarom and the early Omni generations, then begin the playable migration.
- Guide a traveling group through branching wilderness paths, with optional supply caches and route-finding overlooks clearly treated as game invention.
- Reveal Zarahemla from a ridge and descend into the welcoming settlement with the records.

Do not invent detailed revelations or battles for the sparsely described record keepers. Terrain and cache locations are adaptation, not a claim about actual geography.

### 4. Zeniff returns to Lehi-Nephi

**Scripture:** [Omni 1:27–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng), [Mosiah 9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng). A first expedition ends in internal bloodshed and a return. Zeniff leads a second expedition, makes an agreement with King Laman, and repairs and cultivates the settlement; bondage and conflict follow later.

Game variations:

- Traverse the wilderness with the second expedition, choosing paths around obstacles and discovering optional provisions.
- Explore Lehi-Nephi and Shilom through wall walks and streets after the agreement grants settlement.
- Carry building supplies to repair sections of wall or gather seeds for fields, based on the text's rebuilding and cultivation.

Summarize the first expedition's tragedy rather than making the internal fighting entertainment. Do not present Zeniff's overzealous decision as an uncomplicated victory.

### 5. Abinadi's testimony and Alma's escape

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/11?lang=eng), [Mosiah 12–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/12?lang=eng), [Mosiah 17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/17?lang=eng). Noah rules unjustly; Abinadi testifies. Alma believes and pleads for him, is expelled and pursued, hides, and records Abinadi's words. Abinadi is killed for refusing to deny his testimony.

Game variations:

- Explore Noah's city, with its scripturally described towers providing a readable skyline and optional lookout routes.
- After a respectful testimony scene, play Alma's flight through branching streets and wilderness cover.
- Find concealment, then end with Alma preserving Abinadi's words while hidden.

The exact escape architecture is invented. Do not create a successful rescue of Abinadi or turn his death into a playable spectacle.

### 6. Waters of Mormon and the flight to Helam

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?lang=eng), [Mosiah 23:1–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/23?lang=eng). Alma teaches privately near a pure-water fountain and thicket; believers gather and are baptized. Warned of Noah's army, they gather grain and flocks, flee eight days, and settle in Helam.

Game variations:

- Discover the hidden spring through a thicket using several approachable forest routes.
- Help gather grain and flocks for the departure, with light carrying and herding play.
- Lead the wilderness journey to a beautiful land of pure water, ending with a settlement reveal.

Baptism and covenant teaching remain quiet scenes. The escape is about protecting the community, not gaining a combat ability from baptism.

### 7. Benjamin's gathering

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/2?lang=eng), [Mosiah 3–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/3?lang=eng), [Mosiah 6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/6?lang=eng). Families gather in tents facing the temple; Benjamin addresses them from a tower, has his words written for those beyond hearing range, and consecrates Mosiah II.

Game variations:

- Explore a large family encampment with optional service errands and routes between tents.
- Carry written portions of the address to distant groups, directly inspired by Mosiah 2:8.
- Find optional camp overlooks and scenic paths before returning to hear the central address.

Keep the speech, covenant, and taking Christ's name outside score or quiz mechanics. This scene belongs before Ammon finds Limhi, even though the game has already followed the earlier Noah/Alma branch.

### 8. Ammon finds Limhi and the back-pass escape

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/7?lang=eng), [Mosiah 21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/21?lang=eng), [Mosiah 22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/22?lang=eng). Ammon's expedition finds Limhi's people in bondage. Gideon proposes escape by a back pass while the guards are drunk and asleep; the community departs at night and travels around Shilom to Zarahemla.

Game variations:

- Scout the hill north of Shilom and discover a route down toward the city.
- Explore the captive settlement and identify the back pass before gathering possessions and flocks.
- Lead families through the pass and around Shilom on a nighttime escape route with optional safe detours.

The wine tribute is narrative context rather than a drinking mechanic. Include portable precious possessions if treasure gathering is desired: Mosiah 22:12 explicitly mentions gold, silver, and precious things taken on departure. Avoid an invented battle to liberate the city; the text explicitly describes escape instead.

### 9. Helam: burdens and deliverance

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 23:25–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/23?lang=eng), [Mosiah 24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/24?lang=eng). The lost army pursuing Limhi discovers Alma's people. Amulon oppresses them, the Lord strengthens them to bear their burdens, and they depart when their guards are put into a deep sleep. They reach valley Alma and ultimately Zarahemla.

Game variations:

- Gather grain and flocks overnight while preparing the whole community to leave.
- Guide families past the sleeping camp, then traverse wilderness trails with safe alternate paths.
- Reach valley Alma for a quiet thanksgiving scene, followed by the final route toward Zarahemla.

Differentiate this from Limhi's escape visually and mechanically through communal gathering and travel. Do not use prayer as a stealth power meter or imply that the player induces the guards' miraculous sleep.

### 10. Alma the Younger's change

**Scripture:** [Mosiah 27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/27?lang=eng). Alma the Younger and the sons of Mosiah oppose the Church, an angel calls them to account, Alma undergoes conversion, and they then travel to repair harm and declare peace.

Game variations:

- Use a short travel route into a restrained angelic encounter, followed by a quiet recovery scene.
- Revisit settlements to help people and repair damage as an adaptation of their work to repair injuries and publish peace (verses 32–35).
- Let earlier routes gain a new purpose through optional delivery, restoration, and exploration tasks; label the specific errands as game invention.

Do not turn the angel into an enemy, conversion into a collectible reward, or repentance into a timed challenge.

## Strongest next three builds

1. **Enos's forest:** the clearest immediate full adventure after a short Sherem encounter. Forest movement, tracks, logs, rocks, alternate routes, and day-to-night atmosphere suit exploration while leaving prayer reverent.
2. **Mosiah's migration to Zarahemla:** a natural travel adventure with route discovery, an accompanying community, records, and a strong destination reveal. Compress the intervening generations honestly.
3. **Alma's escape and Waters of Mormon:** the strongest later escape-to-discovery arc. Begin with the city flight, follow with the hidden spring, and conclude with gathering and travel toward Helam. It requires the Zeniff/Noah/Abinadi context first and should not skip that context merely to reach the action.

These are build priorities, not permission to rearrange the story: preserve the chronological roadmap and use brief connecting scenes where a standalone level would force unsupported detail.
