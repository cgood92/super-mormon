# Title of Liberty and Mount Antipas

These levels adapt [Alma 46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/46?lang=eng) and [Alma 47](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/47?lang=eng). They retain the recorded outcomes while adapting exploration and traversal for play; condensed chronology is identified below.

## The Title of Liberty

Alma 46:11–13 records Moroni rending his coat, writing on it, fastening it to a pole, arming himself, and praying. Verses 19–22 record his public call and the people running together and making a covenant. Verse 28 says he sent people elsewhere to gather defenders of liberty. Verse 36 says the standard was raised on towers throughout Nephite land. The revised playable route below turns the public call into climbing and exploration, followed by the fixed wilderness outcome.


## Lehonti on Mount Antipas

Alma 47:5–10 identifies Onidah, the gathered army on Mount Antipas, Amalickiah's camp in the valley, and his secret night embassy. Verse 11 gives three successive requests that Lehonti come to the mountain foot; he refuses. Verse 12 says Amalickiah climbs nearly to Lehonti's camp and makes a fourth request, asking him to come down with guards. Verses 13–19 describe the surrounding maneuver, Amalickiah's appointment as second leader, Lehonti's death by poison administered by degrees, and Amalickiah's succession.

The **warning runner, three timed races, spiral trail, quick returns, pennants, and direct warnings to Lehonti are game inventions**. Each race is a full ascent from the trailhead to Lehonti's summit camp; a brief imagined return sets up the next messenger. The messenger actors represent the three recorded embassies; the runner's success never erases their requests or changes Lehonti's decisions. The fourth approach and fatal outcome stay fixed. The physical spiral consists of individually supported, short terraces with tops 0.34 m apart; the player can walk each rise with `stepPlayer`. Fitted central rock masses, outer terraces, and tree trunks use registered collision geometry. Batched terrace scrub, shallow stone facets, pennants, and foliage have explicit nonphysical intent.

`buildTitleOfLiberty(scene)` and `buildLehontiMountain(scene)` return `WorldData` with `adventure` hooks. The city exports `LIBERTY_TOWER_ROUTE`; the mountain exports `ANTIPAS_ROUTE` for route validation and guidance. `tests/super-war-levels.test.ts` exercises the rally, physical tower climb, every mountain ascent, semantic scenery collision, three races, ending, and idempotent disposal. The scene sources use the named cast roles `moroni`, `lehonti`, and `amalickiah` provided by the shared character system.

## Title of Liberty revised play route

Primary source: [Alma 46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/46?lang=eng), especially verses 11–13 (coat, writing, armor, prayer), 19–22 and 28 (waving and armed gathering), 29–33 (march, Amalickiah's escape with a few, remainder taken back to Zarahemla), and 35–37 (covenant, standards, peace). The scene omits the executions in verse 35; no battle objective is offered.

The city's plan, eight rooftop jumping routes, hill stairs, unnamed volunteers, camp location, timing, short acted preparation, and temporary open custody court are invented staging. The record says captives were taken back into the land of Zarahemla; it does not describe a prison at this encounter. Rooftop waving is an exploratory adaptation of Moroni's public call, not a claimed historical route. The final hill raise condenses the later tower standards in verse 36 before the wilderness march for the requested play sequence.

Developer route: start at `(0,0,53)` and walk to prayer court `(-14,0,17)`. Interact once; watch ten seconds of coat removal/rending/writing, prayer and raising. Climb any two distinct building roofs; all eight dwellings count, including west-front `(-47,8,38)`, east-front `(45,9,36)`, west-rear `(-49,8,-19)`. Eight offset solid terraces approach each roof from its inward side; jump from terrace to terrace. Hold action 1.5 seconds on a roof. Repeating the same roof cannot count twice. Take the existing switchback route to `(0,12.8,-65)` and interact. Armed volunteers begin their physical ascent after the first roof call; wait briefly as all twelve gather around Moroni on the summit. They then descend the switchback stairs using `stepPlayer`, and follow Moroni’s ground route down and out to the discoverable wilderness camp `(-69,0,-54)`. Proximity starts an automatic bloodless escape/return sequence with authored escape and returning-captive shots; the ending waits for all four surrendered followers to physically reach custody. Two figures escape; four return to the imagined custody court.

Prologue and ending cameras use the shared safe shot planner; phone and desktop tests check full banner framing, physical camera clearance, and escape/capture/return beats. Tests exercise all eight roofs with actual `stepPlayer` jumps, the full switchback with actual walking, coat/prologue state, distinct-roof counting, summit progression, automatic completion, physical twelve-volunteer summit ascent/descent and corner following, physical captive arrival before completion, semantic scenery collision, and a house-blocked street route. Browser acceptance should inspect the sculpted player costume, torn-cloth inscription, all terrace approaches, late follower descent, sword visibility, camp approach and automatic ending.
