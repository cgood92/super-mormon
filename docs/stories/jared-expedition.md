# Jared's Wilderness Journey

Primary sources: [Ether 1:33–43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/1?lang=eng) and [Ether 2:1–7,13–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/2?lang=eng), consulted October 30, 2026.

This is an explicitly earlier history narrated through Moroni's abridgment. Scripture describes gathered male and female flocks, seed, birds, fish in a prepared vessel, honey bees, wilderness travel, crossings and eventually tents beside the great sea. The shore residence lasted four years; this game's condensed journey does not represent that duration.

Jared's playable shepherding, a single illustrative animal pair, protected provision deliveries, unnamed companions, their speech, costumes, camping architecture, canyon, ridge, hives and shelter are imagined. The sheep-like silhouettes do not assert an identified ancient species. No exact real-world geography is claimed. Proximity guides animals using the same solid collisions as walking; walking ahead slowly keeps them close. A broad central canyon opening and a southern bypass permit planning. Fish and seeds remain attached during transport and return safely after a fall. The three optional places do not gate completion or divine events. Prayer, promised guidance and revelation are contextual history, not abilities or scores.

QA: follow the pair from the western meadow through the central opening to the eastern camp, then return along the same traversable path for the fish and seed loads. Public `jaredExpeditionRoute` provides immutable target coordinates; runtime snapshot exposes copied animal positions, deliveries, discoveries and completion.
