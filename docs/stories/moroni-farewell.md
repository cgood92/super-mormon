# Moroni’s Farewell

Primary sources: [Mormon 8:1–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/8?lang=eng), [Moroni 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/1?lang=eng), [Moroni 10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/10?lang=eng).

The protagonist is Mormon’s son Moroni, not Captain Moroni. He writes alone after his father’s death, abridges the much earlier Jaredite account, continues writing while seeking safety, and closes with invitations to remember mercy and come unto Christ. The game condenses years of wandering; the autumn valley, timber pivot crossing, shelter, costumes, carrier and paths are invented. No exact modern burial geography or invented companion is presented.

Carry one secured inherited record bundle up the eastern stairs. Turn the supported timber into a crossing, walk it to the northern bank, and find a sheltered writing place. Finish by walking a separate western ridge to a hopeful dawn. The lower ravine route and three optional discoveries remain freely explorable. Neither prayer nor conviction is scored, and exploration does not gate sacred invitations. Falls restore the carrier to the last safe landing. The closing view uses the standard gameplay camera and permits free exploration and replay through the gallery.

QA: `MORONI_ROUTE` exports continuous physics routes; snapshot includes carrier ownership, bridge/crossing/writing state, checkpoints and discoveries. The physical timber collider moves with its visible mesh. Main completion requires actual bank crossing followed by writing and dawn navigation; idle interactions cannot finish.
