# Mormon and the Hidden Records

Primary sources: [Mormon 1:1–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/1?lang=eng), [Mormon 2:17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/2?lang=eng), [Mormon 4:23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/4?lang=eng), and [Mormon 6:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/6?lang=eng).

Ammaron spoke to Mormon when Mormon was ten, asking him to remember the hill and later take the plates of Nephi while leaving the other records. Mormon 2:17 describes taking those plates. The playable retrieval is explicitly MANY YEARS LATER: adult Mormon takes all the records from Shim during the withdrawal in 4:23. Ammaron is a contextual memory, not a living companion in this scene. The much-later Cumorah epilogue distinguishes most records hidden by Mormon from a few plates entrusted to his son Moroni.

The valley, hill form, refuges, costumes, unnamed travelers, tied carrier, paths, spillway, architectural details and all optional discoveries are invented. Scripture does not identify this geography. The many-watered green atmosphere is an artistic interpretation; the many-waters description in Mormon 6:4 concerns Cumorah, not proof of Shim's surroundings. One portable carrier represents the preservation journey, not an assertion about the complete records' weight or transport method. No battle outcome can be changed.

Play: leave the open north face of the refuge, climb either broad stair, enter the archive from its southern doorway and lift the carrier. Return down the east stair. At the spillway, amber spray warns before the three-second surge; wait on dry ground or take the wide east bank. A surge or fall returns player and carrier together to the last dry checkpoint. Carrying requires both hands, so a ladder grab with the carrier safely returns to the checkpoint with an explanation. No optional discovery gates completion. After shelter delivery, the later Cumorah account is brief and exploration remains available.

Optional discoveries: spring grotto west of the hill; abandoned western terrace; eastern archive overlook. Both sheltered rooms are accessible. Foreground rocks, ridges, trunks, walls, stair treads and ladder share visible dimensions with physical support. Natural buttresses use fitted shared rock geometry. Repeated soft fern banks are instanced; disposal releases their instance buffers and all geometry/materials. Standard gameplay camera remains in use.
