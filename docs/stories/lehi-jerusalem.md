# Lehi warns Jerusalem and returns home

Primary source: [1 Nephi 1:4, 18–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/1?lang=eng&id=p4,p18-p20#p18). Lehi prophesies among the people; they mock his testimony, become angry and seek his life. The opening paraphrases his warning and the response without presenting invented speech as a quotation from scripture.

The fifteen-second opening is an artistic reconstruction: the streets, buildings, clothing, unnamed listeners, gestures, immediate chase home and blue door are imagined gameplay staging. Scripture does not specify this escape route or the color of Lehi’s door. The actors’ appearance does not identify righteousness or wickedness; hostility is expressed through their response and pursuit.

Four edited shots show Lehi’s warning (five seconds), the crowd’s response (four), the blue door (three), and Lehi turning toward the escape (three). Captions communicate the story without requiring audio. The normal player avatar and city remain in use; cameras are planned against physical solids and opaque render geometry for the current aspect ratio. Edited cuts avoid sweeping through intervening houses. The last shot returns to Lehi before the follow camera resumes.

Player movement, pursuit time and capture grace remain held during the opening. Normal chase gameplay begins afterward, with the existing reachable outer-west-lane route and home completion scene. Capture retries return to the starting street without replaying the opening; restarting the chapter plays it again. The scene-testing entry does not write story progress.
