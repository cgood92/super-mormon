# Light upon the Waters

Primary sources: [Ether 2:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/2?lang=eng) and [Ether 6:2–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/6?lang=eng).

Ether describes tight vessels with closable openings, two shining stones in each vessel, provisions for people and animals, wind carrying the vessels, repeated submersion, praise and arrival after 344 days. This adventure explicitly condenses those 344 days. The wind and arrival are never controlled by steering, sails, a rudder or player-created weather. Two stones continue shining during every storm phase.

The vessel interior, cutaway roof for camera visibility, raised service aisle, feed troughs, secured basket, family positions, gentle storm cycle and safety cover are invented playable staging, not a construction reconstruction. The cutaway is not a window. Visible side walls, hull ribs and animal rails block passage. The player carries a loose basket into a lashing bay, carries feed to a reachable trough, climbs the east work stairs, opens the air lid during a calm interval, and closes it. Amber warning precedes submersion. Missing an interval closes the safety cover and allows another attempt without drowning or lost cargo. There is no inverted camera or moving physical floor.

After these care activities the passage of condensed time leads to a source-labeled arrival scene and opens the shore hatch. Walking onto the beach completes once and leaves free beach/vessel exploration. Family reflection, storage and a supported coast lookout are optional. No precise ancient geography is asserted. `CROSSING_TARGETS` and snapshots expose work locations, phase, safe ventilation and the 344-day context for QA.
