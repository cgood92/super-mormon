# Alma war adventures: source and staging notes

- **Morianton's Flight** follows [Alma 50:25–36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/50?lang=eng). The maidservant reports Morianton's plan; Moroni sends Teancum; Teancum intercepts the company at the narrow pass by the sea on both sides; Morianton dies in battle; the remaining people covenant to keep peace and return home. The exact coastline, scouts, boulders, and timed route are invented for play. The maidservant's mistreatment is reported without a visual reenactment.
- **Teancum's Night Mission** follows [Alma 51:32–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng). Teancum and his servant go out at night, enter Amalickiah's sleeping camp, Teancum uses a javelin, and they return to wake and prepare their own men. The playable patrols and tent placement are imagined. The death occurs in a brief non-graphic cutaway. This is Amalickiah in Alma 51, not the later Ammoron mission in Alma 62.
- **The Rescue at Gid** follows [Alma 55:4–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/55?lang=eng). Laman, a descendant of Laman, takes wine to the guards; when they sleep he reports to Moroni; Moroni's men cast weapons over the walls to the prisoners, including women and children able to use them; Moroni surrounds the city, the captains surrender, and the prisoners are freed without bloodshed. The supply caches, two throw points, and city layout are invented gameplay details. Rope ladders belong to the separate Nephihah account in Alma 62:18–26, not this Gid account.
