# Lehonti on Mount Antipas

Primary source: [Alma 47](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/47?lang=eng), especially verses 5–13 and 18–19.

The opening establishes Lehonti's summit camp (verse 7), his people's refusal to fight the Nephites (verse 6), and Amalickiah's ambition to command the armies and dethrone the king (verse 8). Amalickiah camps in the nearby valley and sends a secret embassy at night asking Lehonti to come down (verses 9–10). Lehonti refuses three messages (verse 11).

The player is an invented warning runner. The three timed warning races, spoken narration, gestures, spiral terraces, costumes, camp layout, vegetation and daylight staging are invented or condensed for play. Scripture does not say that a runner warned Lehonti or caused his refusals. The opening labels the warning race as imagined, and its clock starts only after the movie returns to the player at the trailhead.

The three successful warnings recall the first three refusals. They do not change the later scriptural outcome: Amalickiah eventually comes nearly to the camp, Lehonti comes down with his guards (verses 12–13), and is subsequently poisoned (verses 18–19). The game concludes this episode before those later events. Mount Antipas's exact geography and the visible reconstruction are not established historical geography.

The opening uses edited summit, valley and runner shots rather than flying through the mountain. Shared camera planning checks solid terrain and opaque rendered scenery, with phone and desktop framing. Movement and messenger progress remain frozen during the 16-second opening. Retries and subsequent races start immediately at the trailhead without replaying the opening.
