# The Sixteen Stones of Shelem

Primary sources: [Ether 3:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng) and [Ether 6:2–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/6?lang=eng).

Ether describes sixteen small clear stones molten from rock, the exceedingly high mountain Shelem, the brother of Jared's prayer and the Lord touching the stones. Eight vessels later receive one shining stone at each end. The playable ascent begins with all sixteen stones already prepared together. There is no collection hunt, faith score or timing requirement for the miracle. At the summit the light is witnessed in a fixed quiet interval; the divine appearance is not depicted. The player then carries the light down a separate east stair route.

The secure tray, costume, mountain shape, zigzag stairs, ladders, wind warning and shelter checkpoints, sea view, spring, high saddle and Jared waiting below are invented staging. They are not a reconstruction or a claim about a known location. The sheltered stairs are always available; the narrow west shortcut is optional and amber wind warns before a gust returns the intact carrier to the last sheltered terrace. Optional discoveries never gate the sacred event.

QA: `SHELEM_ROUTE` exports the continuous main ascent, east return and three discovery locations; snapshots expose all sixteen stones, carrier, fixed witness interval, light, checkpoint and optional finds. Standard gameplay camera with no authored cinematic camera override. All physical surfaces use shared scenery geometry/collision; the single ground, stair treads and terraces own their respective support. Resource disposal is idempotent and the clock clamps long/invalid frames.
