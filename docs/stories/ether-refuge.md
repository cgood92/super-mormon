# Ether’s Refuge

Primary sources: [Ether 13:13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/13?lang=eng), [Ether 15:33–34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/15?lang=eng).

Ether lived centuries after Jared and his brother. The text describes sheltering in a cave, observing events at night and recording them by day, and ultimately placing the finished account where others could find it. The game condenses this period into two distant observation routes and a nighttime return to writing. It does not allow the destruction to be prevented or show graphic battle.

The cave, three entrances, ruins, lookout stairs, patrol, awareness lantern, costumes, gardens, route choices and all spoken/textual paraphrases are invented adaptations. No archaeological identification is asserted. Ether is alone: water cavern, skylight court and abandoned garden replace friendly crowds. Observations survive fair refuge retries. Cover screens both side routes; crouching reduces exposure in the open court. No trophies, kills or manuscript-fragment fetching.

QA: `ETHER_ROUTE` exports continuous walking itineraries; the readable runtime snapshot includes observations, discoveries, awareness and recovery count. The two lookout stair routes require actual supported ascent and return. Completion requires both observations and interaction back in the refuge, then leaves exploration open.
