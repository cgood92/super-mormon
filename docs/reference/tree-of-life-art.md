# Tree of Life gallery illustration

Asset: [public/art/tree.png](../../public/art/tree.png). Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then copied into the project. It is an original illustrative painting, not a reconstruction of an actual location.

Final prompt:

> Create an original portrait 3:4 fine illustrative painting to hang as the entrance to a reverent Book of Mormon 3D game chapter, no text, no frame. Lehi's dream, 1 Nephi 8: a large radiant spreading Tree of Life with beautiful pale leaves and distinctly WHITE fruit on a verdant meadow at left-center, inviting and peaceful, not garish. An iron handrail follows a narrow winding path from foreground through soft dark mists toward the tree. A few small dignified ancient Near Eastern travelers in linen robes hold the rod. Nearby a clear spring and garden, further to the right a deep rocky gulf and dark river separate the tree from an elevated magnificent many-balconied ancient building with tiny elegantly dressed people pointing mockingly from it. Warm luminous light around tree contrasts distant misty cooler building, broad dream landscape with mountains and flowers, thoughtful sacred atmosphere. Ancient people around 600 BC, original painterly polished narrative illustration with softly simplified forms compatible with a low-poly adventure; no Nintendo assets, coins, game UI, comedy, weapons or modern objects.

The playable landscape is an artistic interpretation of the symbols described in [1 Nephi 8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/8?lang=eng), with explanations from chapters 11, 12 and 15. Its placement, proportions and exploration paths are invented.
