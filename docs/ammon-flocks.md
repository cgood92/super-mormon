# Ammon and the flocks at Sebus

Primary source: [Alma 17:19–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/17?lang=eng).

Verses 25–27 describe Ammon serving among Lamoni’s servants, taking the flocks to Sebus to water, and men scattering them. Verses 31–34 describe gathering them back to the water, the servants encircling them, and Ammon going to contend with the men. Verse 36 describes the sling and the attackers’ unsuccessful stones. The encounter ends violently in verses 36–39; this game abridges the violence and depicts the attackers withdrawing, without wounds or graphic detail.

The player begins after Ammon has entered service. Herding all six animals into the visible shallows and a short drinking pause precede an acted scatter sequence. Animals are recovered once; servants then hold them while Ammon uses his sling. Returning the flock concludes the playable service.

The water’s shape, landscape, buildings, flock species/count, three attackers, masks and dark clothes, dialogue, cinematic choreography, timing, and controls are illustrative inventions. Dark costumes and masks identify gameplay roles, not a historical Lamanite uniform. Both Ammon’s fellow servants and the attackers are Lamanites in this account; skin color and ancestry do not communicate allegiance. No specific modern location is asserted for Sebus.
