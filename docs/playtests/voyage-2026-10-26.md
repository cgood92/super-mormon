# Across the Great Waters playtest corrections

Primary source: [1 Nephi 18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng), especially verses 9–23. Scripture describes the brothers' rudeness, binding, the fourth day of the tempest, their repentance and release of Nephi, his praise and prayer, the working compass, calm, renewed guidance, and arrival after many days. It does not identify the crossing route, rocky islands, ship architecture, wheel, stairs, clothing, dialogue, or a visiting child. Those remain invented game framing; the passage is a navigation challenge, not a claim about historical geography. The opening condenses the earlier binding and the fourth day. The prayer/Liahona interactions condense verse 21 rather than claiming an exact reconstruction of its chronology.

The release actors previously interpolated height directly toward the upper waypoint; they now use the same stair collision and step simulation as the child. Both mast trunks and the bound character block walking. Release switches directly into Nephi's prayer objective, retaining the child as a visible companion; there is no additional stand-with-Nephi action.

Sailing uses one continuous sea coordinate system. Islands and distant shore share the translation and heading transform. Clearance uses the full 18 by 44 ship footprint and conservative island geometry bounds, rather than the ship center. The wider channel provides room for the hull, with checkpoints only after the stern clears a passage. A contact restores the last safe location and heading; it does not advance the route. One journey percentage replaces five separate success episodes. Rocky passages are imagined challenges with fair retries.

Browser review found the original stern camera let opaque square sails cover the steering view. The final approach now uses a raised external view and visibly reefed sails high on the yards (invented vessel handling, not a detail supplied by scripture). Regression checks raycast actual rendered ship meshes toward the island crest, channel marker and representative bow surfaces, and project the complete hull footprint plus the next marker at phone and desktop aspect ratios.
