# Finding Sherem

Primary sources: [Jacob 7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng) and [2 Nephi 5:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/5?lang=eng&id=p16#p16).

Jacob 7:1 places Sherem's arrival after some years. Verses 6–15 describe his challenge, Jacob's witness, the requested sign, and Sherem falling under the Lord's power. He is cared for for many days. He then requests a gathering the next day, confesses Christ, and dies after speaking (verses 15–20). Peace returns among the people (verse 23).

The player explores as Jacob, passing the interpretive temple before reaching the city and outer gardens. Finding a moving Sherem and speaking beside him starts one short encounter. After the dialogue his fall is acted without violence, attacks, flashes, or rewards. An automatic closing caption remains for eight seconds before completion, with no further action required. Its time jump explicitly describes the subsequent care, next-day confession, and death; the fall itself does not portray immediate death. There are no house-of-care, witness, or community-walk chores.

The settlement layout, temple placement, plum mantle, unnamed inhabitants, patrols, player search, dialogue paraphrases, and condensed staging are inventions for exploration. Scripture says Sherem sought Jacob; having the player find him is an interactive framing, not a claim that Jacob historically searched for him. Existing cast models supply varied unnamed residents; their model roles do not imply that Sam, Sariah, or other earlier named people were present. Terrain and architecture are not claims about known Nephite geography or excavated buildings.

The actor's current position controls interaction. No beacon identifies him. Residents walk along open streets and outskirts with matching moving collision bodies; solid houses remain explorable through doors. The reused landscape batches plants and disposes instances, and the extra residents use crowd-quality rigs.
